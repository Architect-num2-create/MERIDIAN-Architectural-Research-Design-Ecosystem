# Community Branch

This is the organ that has to survive contact with a stranger — a future student who forks Layer 5 having read none of this dossier's internal history.

**The invitation.** Take the transformation method itself, not the four sources: decompose sources into logics, not styles; keep one source's plan as an unbroken constraint, the way this project never touched Saint Peter's cruciform footprint; let the friction between two incompatible sources generate the form, rather than designing the form first and justifying it afterward. Peter, Basil, Ilkhanid, and Achaemenid are not the point. The method of deciding what to keep whole and what to let collide is the only thing a fork is actually required to inherit.

**What this organ owes a fork, honestly.** Per the project's own stated plan, Layer 5 will not open until Dossiers I through III have been battle-tested — meaning a fork arriving today would inherit whatever blind spots Organ II hasn't caught yet. Before this organ accepts a single fork, its first real job is to make Organ II's findings mandatory reading, not a note buried in a README that a motivated stranger can skip past.

**What a fork is asked to add back.** Every fork that survives long enough to matter should be required to write its own Organ II — its own contradiction map, its own blind spots — before it's allowed to open a Layer 5 of its own. An ecosystem that only inherits confidence and never inherits self-suspicion stops being this project's method and becomes something more fragile wearing its name.
