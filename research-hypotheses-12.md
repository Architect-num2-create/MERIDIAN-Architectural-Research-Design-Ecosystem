# 12 — HYPOTHESIS LAB

A hypothesis, in this dossier, is a claim MERIDIAN is willing to make out
loud, with a stated path toward confirming or discarding it — distinct from
a [Research Gap](../11_RESEARCH_GAPS/), which is a question not yet
seriously attempted, and distinct from a [Design Decision](../08_EVIDENCE_SYSTEM/)
(D2), which the project has simply chosen and does not need to prove.

## Active hypotheses

## Discipline

A hypothesis stays in this file, honestly labeled H1 or X1, for as long as
it is genuinely unresolved. It should never quietly migrate into
[Dossier III — Paper](../../03_PAPER/) worded as settled fact. When a
hypothesis is confirmed with real evidence, it graduates to
[Knowledge Extraction](../07_KNOWLEDGE_EXTRACTION/) as an operator or to
[Historical Intelligence](../04_HISTORICAL_INTELLIGENCE/) as a fact. When it
is disconfirmed, it moves to this file's resolved table, not deleted — a
tested and rejected hypothesis is still part of the record of how the
project thought.
