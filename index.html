Architectural research & design ecosystem

MERIDIAN

History → Transformation → Emergence

A single architectural artifact operating simultaneously as a design proposition, a research archive, a documented method, and a published argument — investigating how historical architecture can become active material for a new architectural language, without reproducing the visual identity of its sources.

Status — Active research & development Author — Parham Mirzaei Sanatgar Institution — Tehran Azad University

“How can history become an active architectural material through a disciplined process of extraction, transformation, synthesis and emergence, so that a new architectural language can arise without reproducing the visual identity of its sources?” — The core question MERIDIAN works from
