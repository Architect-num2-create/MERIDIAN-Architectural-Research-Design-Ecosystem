DOSSIER I — RESEARCH / INVESTIGATION

> "The laboratory doors are open."

This is not a bibliography. A bibliography shows what was read. This dossier
shows how MERIDIAN came to know what it knows — the questions that were asked,
the sources that were interrogated, the contradictions that surfaced, the
paths that were tried and abandoned, and the gaps that remain open on
purpose.

Everything downstream depends on this folder. Dossier II (Design) does not
invent architecture from taste; it converts what is verified, extracted, or
knowingly hypothesized here into spatial and formal decisions. If a design
decision in Dossier II cannot be traced back to something in this dossier —
a source, a question, an extracted principle, or a named hypothesis — that
decision has not yet earned its place in the project.

## Documentation principle

Every substantive claim in this dossier should be traceable through:

`Origin → Evidence → Interpretation → Operation → Transformation → Decision → Result`

Not every entry needs every link filled in yet — that is what
[Research Gaps](11_RESEARCH_GAPS/) is for — but the chain should never be
silently skipped.

## What does not belong here

- Finished architectural decisions (those live in Dossier II — Design/Construction)
- The public-facing scholarly argument (Dossier III — Paper)
- Speculative tangents with no research discipline behind them (those belong
  in Dossier IV — Dark Forest, once they are honestly labeled as speculation)
