# Reading Without Explanation, and the Future Reader

Two postures, both deliberately built into this dossier — not accidental gaps in it.

**Reading without explanation.** Someone who opens the Case Anatomy cold, with none of the Manifesto's framing, should still feel that something is being concealed and revealed on purpose — the backlit LEDs, the hidden corridors, the private name — without being told which parts are the concealment. This dossier is written so the concealment is legible as a *shape*, even to a reader who never gets the key. That is a harder standard than clarity. Clarity explains itself; this only has to be honest about the fact that it isn't explaining itself.

**The future reader.** Someone finding this ten years from now, after the defense is long over, the repo possibly forked past recognition, the physical board maybe actually framed under glass on a university wall. To that reader, directly: the numbers were chosen, not discovered. You are not meant to find a lost meaning here — you are meant to decide whether the meaning that was put here still holds, for you, in whatever the world has become by then. That is a different, harder task than decoding a puzzle, and it is the one this dossier was actually built for.
