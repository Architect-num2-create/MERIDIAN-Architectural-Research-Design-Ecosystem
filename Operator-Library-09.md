# MERIDIAN — Operator Library

These are not effects.

They are actions on architecture.

The first group is already visible in the project.

### SPLIT

One mass becomes more than one condition.

But the split should create a new relationship.

Otherwise it is just a seam.

### AGGREGATE

Separate parts are brought into a larger order.

The interesting question is what holds them together.

### OFFSET

A part moves away from the expected alignment.

This can create tension, entry, asymmetry or a new view.

### ROTATE

A geometry changes orientation.

Rotation becomes useful when it changes movement, structure, light or the relationship between systems.

### LAYER

One condition sits over or behind another.

Layering can be spatial, structural, material or atmospheric.

### STEP

A continuous form becomes a sequence of levels.

The step can change scale, light, section, structure or movement.

### OPEN

Solid becomes penetrable.

Opening is not just a window.

It can be a cut, courtyard, threshold, shaft, void or sectional exposure.

### EXPOSE

Something previously hidden becomes readable.

Structure can be exposed.

A lower level can be revealed.

A void can appear only after movement.

### RADIALIZE

A linear or orthogonal relationship begins to organize around a centre.

This is especially important because MERIDIAN already contains both axial and radial tendencies.

### DIFFERENTIATE

Repeated elements stop being identical.

Difference can be controlled through height, scale, geometry, material, rhythm or position.

### CONVERGE

Separate trajectories move toward one condition.

This can happen in plan, section, structure or circulation.

### COMPRESS

Space, distance, height or light is reduced.

Compression prepares release.

### RELEASE

The opposite.

A tight condition opens into scale, light, height or view.

### REVERSE

An expected relationship is inverted.

Heavy becomes light.

Centre becomes void.

Peripheral elements become dominant.

An entrance can become an interior space.

This is dangerous but powerful when justified.

### RECOMBINE

Elements that have been separated are brought together under a new rule.

Not collage.

New relationship.

---

The library should stay small.

If every verb becomes an operator, the word "operator" loses meaning.

A useful operator is one that can be applied to a real architectural condition and produce a different architectural consequence.
