# 11 — RESEARCH GAPS

A gap, here, means something specific: a question the project needs
answered, that is not yet answered, and that would change something if it
were. It does not mean "a number without a historical citation" — see
[Research Matrix](../10_RESEARCH_MATRIX/) for why that is a settled design
decision, not a gap.

## Open gaps, ranked by consequence

**Blocking — affects claims made throughout the dossier**

- The precise structural logic of the 185-column / arch rhythm along the
  courtyard walls has not been stated. If a module, arc-length, or
  proportional logic exists, it should be documented here; if the number is
  simply a design decision with no internal derivation, that should be
  stated just as plainly — an undocumented derivation left silent is worse
  than an honestly stated design decision.
- The kinetic dome mechanism's actual mode of operation — actuation, power
  source, control logic, safety behavior — is undefined. Every downstream
  claim about "light as architecture" and "time" depends on this being
  resolved at least to the level of an architectural hypothesis, even if
  full engineering resolution is out of scope for this phase.

**Critical — could materially change a design decision**

- Structural plausibility of the zigguratic dome (stepped, terraced,
  circular) has not been checked against any comparable built or engineered
  precedent. This is currently held as H1 in the
  [Hypothesis Lab](../12_HYPOTHESIS_LAB/).
- No specific Ilkhanid monument yet demonstrates a threshold principle
  recurring at more than one physical scale within a single building. Until
  found, this claim stays labeled I1 (authorial interpretation), not F-grade
  historical fact.
- The transformation from the Ilkhanid four-iwan courtyard (four arms) and
  the Achaemenid linear sequence into MERIDIAN's two-arm convergent
  courtyard has not yet been argued step by step in Dossier II. The
  operators are extracted; the synthesis is not yet written down.

**Important — strengthens or weakens the argument, not load-bearing**

- Whether double-shell construction knowledge actually travelled from
  Ilkhanid Iran to Renaissance Italy is a genuine, unresolved
  historiographical debate. MERIDIAN does not need to resolve it, but
  should state clearly that it is citing Soltaniyeh's priority, not a
  transmission claim, per [Iranian Architectural Position](../06_IRANIAN_ARCHITECTURAL_POSITION/).
- Reader-experience and cognitive-engagement claims (attention, curiosity,
  pattern recognition) currently rest on general cognitive science rather
  than anything specific to architectural experience or to this project.
  See [Knowledge Limitations](../13_KNOWLEDGE_LIMITATIONS/).
- No kinetic architecture precedent exists at dome-integrated aperture
  scale; the nearest analogues (retractable stadium roofs, rotating floors,
  shading façades) operate at the building envelope, not inside a dome
  structure. This bounds, but does not eliminate, the mechanism's
  plausibility.

## What closes a gap

A gap closes when either (a) genuine historical or technical evidence is
found and graded per the [Evidence System](../08_EVIDENCE_SYSTEM/), or (b)
the project makes a deliberate design decision and labels it D2, honestly,
in the relevant file. A gap does not close by simply not mentioning it
again.
