# MERIDIAN — Variables

The project becomes design when something is allowed to move.

Not everything should move.

Some things hold the architecture together.

The rest are variables.

A variable is useful only when changing it changes the building in a meaningful way.

### Mass

How much is one body?

How much can it be separated?

Where does separation become another building instead of another part of the same one?

### Axis

The axis can be straight, interrupted, doubled, rotated, displaced or weakened.

But every change should affect movement or hierarchy.

### Centre

The centre can be compressed, enlarged, raised, opened, buried or made void.

The interesting question is not how large it is.

It is what happens to the rest of the building when the centre changes.

### Peripheral field

The outside elements can become:

closer,

further,

taller,

lower,

denser,

looser,

more similar,

more different.

This is where multiplicity can become spatial rather than graphic.

### Void

The void can be central, linear, nested, cut through the mass, buried, open to the sky or only partially visible.

A void is a variable when changing it changes light, circulation, proportion, climate or social use.

### Height

Height can establish hierarchy.

It can also hide hierarchy.

A lower peripheral field can make the centre monumental.

A higher peripheral field can make the centre feel embedded.

### Section

The section can expose relationships that the plan hides.

Layering, stepping, compression, release and vertical overlap belong here.

### Curvature

Curvature should not be used because it makes the form richer.

It should change how bodies meet, how people move, how light travels or how structure works.

### Rhythm

Rhythm can be regular, interrupted, accelerated, slowed or broken.

The break may be more important than the repetition.

### Proportion

Proportion is where intuition becomes difficult to fake.

A proportion that feels right but cannot yet be explained should stay open rather than receiving a fake mathematical story.

### Surface

Pattern, striation, relief and colour belong late.

They can become powerful when they continue a deeper spatial or structural logic.

They are weak when they are asked to rescue a weak mass.

---

The point is not to create a giant parameter list.

The point is to know which levers actually move MERIDIAN.
