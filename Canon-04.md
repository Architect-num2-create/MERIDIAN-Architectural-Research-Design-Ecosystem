# CANON

These don't get re-litigated. Changing any of them takes a Constitution amendment, not an edit.

1. The plan is Saint Peter's cruciform footprint, unaltered.
2. Seven stepped ziggurat domes — one large, six small, three tiers each. None carries a cross, lantern, spire, or onion.
3. Five small domes read Saint Basil; two read Ilkhanid.
4. Five structural columns quote Achaemenid Persepolis.
5. ~185 courtyard columns in a column–arch–column rhythm with Ilkhanid arches.
6. The courtyard has two curved arms, stepping up toward the main volume, with hidden corridors inside the walls.
7. The Aperture Core is a light mechanism at the crossing — its exact form stays deliberately cross-era plausible, not locked to one literal spec.
8. The dome and column counts are symbolic, chosen for meaning — not derived from historical precedent or load calculation, and not to be corrected against either.
9. The original sheet was submitted unfinished and received 20/20.
10. Author of record: Parham Mirzaei, Tehran Azad University. Any claim of authorship elsewhere is false.

Everything not on this list is still moving.
