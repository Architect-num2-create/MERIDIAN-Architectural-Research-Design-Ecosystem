# Failure Atlas, Counterfactuals, Alternate Rings, Dark Branches

### Failure atlas
Real ways this project could have died, logged the way a case file logs near-misses.

- **F1.** If the professor's simpler artistic-copy grouping had been accepted instead of refused, there would be no engineering layer at all — a decorative sheet, none of the structural ambition that makes Dossier III possible today.
- **F2.** Stable Diffusion and other image-generators were tried and failed to merge the four historical references. If they had worked, the sheet might have leaned on a synthesized render instead of a hand-built diagrammatic one — and the diagrammatic, process-revealing presentation that an AI assistant and, separately, a professor both suggested might never have happened. The tool's failure produced the object's actual identity.
- **F3.** If the sheet had been finished on time, there is no live case of "an incomplete object judged complete" — arguably the single most interesting fact in the whole case (see Organ I) would not exist.
- **F4.** If Layer 5 opens to forks before Dossiers I through III are battle-tested, the first forks inherit the project's current blind spots as permanent scaffolding, before anyone catches them.

### Counterfactuals
Small changes, traced forward.

- **CF1.** Suppose the numerology had been derived from real historical column counts instead of chosen for meaning. C1 resolves — the project gains defensibility — and it loses the "encoded by a legendary hidden architect" narrative entirely. The mystery and the rigor were never fully compatible; this is the clearest proof of that trade.
- **CF2.** Suppose the Aperture Core had been given one literal, era-specific engineering spec instead of cross-era flexible language. It becomes gradeable by an engineer and unreadable as myth by everyone else — the same trade as CF1, showing up independently in a second part of the project. That repetition is itself a finding: MERIDIAN keeps re-encountering the choice between being provable and being legendary, in a different costume each time.

### Alternate rings
Versions of the Aperture Core that were not chosen, kept here rather than discarded, in case a future reader or a fork wants to pick one up: a version driven by seasonal solar angle instead of a rotating column; a version where the ring stays fixed but the aperture material itself changes translucency; a version with no mechanism at all, where the seven domes' fixed oculi do across the day what a mechanism was meant to do across its motion.

### Dark branches
Threads opened and deliberately not pursued — seeds, not dead ends: what the courtyard's hidden corridors would mean if a visitor could discover them, rather than their existing only in the architect's knowledge; whether the "special" students who took the easier, artistic-copy path ever produced something worth studying as a genuine counter-case, instead of being dismissed as the road not taken.
