# 13 — KNOWLEDGE LIMITATIONS

Every research method has a boundary past which it cannot honestly speak.
Naming that boundary is not a weakness in MERIDIAN — pretending it doesn't
exist would be.

## What historical and art-historical research cannot settle

- **Intent versus interpretation.** Reading the Apadana's lion-and-bull
  reliefs as seasonal symbolism, or the four-iwan sequence as a spiritual
  threshold, is scholarly interpretation of physical and pictorial
  evidence — not a record of what any historical patron or builder actually
  intended. MERIDIAN can use these readings, graded honestly as F3 or I1,
  but should never present them as recovered intent.
- **Disputed transmission claims.** Whether double-shell construction
  knowledge travelled from Ilkhanid Iran to Renaissance Italy is a live,
  unresolved historiographical debate. This research method can report the
  dispute; it cannot resolve it. MERIDIAN's position rests on Soltaniyeh's
  documented priority, which is a different and more defensible claim than
  a transmission claim.
- **The limits of comparison across radically different periods and
  scales.** Comparing a 6th-century-BCE ceremonial terrace, a 14th-century
  mausoleum, a 16th-century cathedral, and a 21st-century student project is
  useful for extracting principles, but it cannot establish that these
  buildings "belong to the same tradition" in any stronger sense. The
  comparison is MERIDIAN's own analytical construction.

## What this research method cannot tell us about structure and engineering

- Historical and archival research can establish that the Soltaniyeh dome
  exists and stands; it cannot, by itself, establish that MERIDIAN's
  zigguratic dome is structurally sound. That requires actual engineering
  analysis, not analogy to a differently-shaped precedent. This is why
  structural hypotheses stay labeled H1 in the
  [Hypothesis Lab](../12_HYPOTHESIS_LAB/) rather than being upgraded on the
  strength of historical comparison alone.
- The same applies, more strongly, to the kinetic dome mechanism: no amount
  of historical or precedent research can substitute for an actual
  mechanical feasibility study.

## What this research method cannot tell us about the reader

Claims about attention, curiosity, or "the mind staying engaged" belong to
cognitive and perceptual science, and general findings in that field are
not automatically true of architectural experience, let alone of this
specific project. Until MERIDIAN commissions or cites study of actual
readers or viewers engaging with this specific material, claims of this
kind should be held as aspiration and design intention, not demonstrated
effect. This limitation is tracked as an open item in
[Research Gaps](../11_RESEARCH_GAPS/), not resolved here.

## Why this file exists at all

A project that argues for intellectual rigor has to apply that rigor to
itself first. This file exists so that no claim in
[Dossier III — Paper](../../03_PAPER/) accidentally claims more than this
research method is actually capable of establishing.
