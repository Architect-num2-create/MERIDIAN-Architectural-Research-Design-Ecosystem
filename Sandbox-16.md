# SANDBOX

A place to break things without breaking Canon.

Anything can be tried here — a fifth historical source thrown against the existing four just to see what collides, a wild version of the Aperture Core that doesn't survive five minutes of the Skeleton's math, a reading of the Contradiction Map that argues C1 shouldn't exist at all. None of it needs an epistemic tag. None of it needs to be defensible yet.

The only rule: nothing moves from here into Experimental or Canon without going through the real chain — SRC through RES — like everything else. The Sandbox is where ideas are allowed to be embarrassing. It's not a side door around the provenance requirement.
