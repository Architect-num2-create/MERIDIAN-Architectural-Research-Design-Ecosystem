# ONTOLOGY

**Source.** A thing outside MERIDIAN that MERIDIAN looked at — a building, a paper, a photograph, a museum record. Doesn't belong to MERIDIAN. Gets an SRC-### the moment it's used for anything.

**Evidence.** What a Source actually shows, before anyone decides what it means. An EVD-### never contains an opinion.

**Claim.** A sentence built on top of Evidence. Tagged F1 through R1 depending on how much weight it can hold.

**Interpretation.** What MERIDIAN thinks a Claim means architecturally. Always tagged I1 — it's mine, not history's.

**Hypothesis.** A guess dressed up enough to be testable.

**Decision.** The moment a Hypothesis, Interpretation, or plain instinct actually changed the building. Has to name what it rejected, not just what it chose.

**Transformation.** The operation that turned an old logic into a new one — abstraction, inversion, fragmentation, and so on. This is the actual verb of the whole project; everything upstream of it is just setup.

**Experiment.** A Transformation, tested.

**Result.** What an Experiment actually produced, whether or not it was what was expected.

**Rejection.** Something that almost got in and didn't. Kept forever, never deleted — a Rejection is data, not trash.

**Open Problem.** A Question nobody's answered yet, kept alive on purpose.

**Dossier.** One of the five bodies MERIDIAN is made of — Research, Design, Paper, Dark Forest, Core. Each thinks differently. None is allowed to fully agree with the others.

**Organ.** A working part inside a Dossier — the Contradiction Map is an organ of Dark Forest, the Constitution is an organ of Core.

**Canon.** Something that's stopped being a Hypothesis. Once it's Canon, changing it takes an Amendment, not an edit.

**Contradiction.** Two true things in MERIDIAN that can't both be fully true at once. Not a bug. Tracked on purpose.

**Fork.** Someone else's MERIDIAN — running the same method on different material, connected to this one by lineage, not by ownership.

**Contributor.** A human who added a Source, Claim, Transformation, or Fork and put their name on it.

**Reader.** Anyone who shows up later with no stake in any of this. Everything here is eventually written for them.
