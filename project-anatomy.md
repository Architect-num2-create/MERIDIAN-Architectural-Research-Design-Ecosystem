# MERIDIAN — Project Anatomy

This is not the explanation of MERIDIAN.

It is what is physically and visibly there before we explain it.

The project begins with a mass.

That mass is not kept whole.

It is split, broken into parts, aggregated again, and pushed toward a cross-based organization. In parallel, vertical mass is layered, opened directionally, curved radially, and exposed through section.

The sheet itself shows this as a sequence rather than a single jump:

primary mass  
→ axial split  
→ modular aggregation  
→ cross-based configuration

and:

layered vertical mass  
→ directional openings  
→ radial curving  
→ sectional exposure

That matters.

The project was not born as "a building with several historical details". It was born through changes to mass, organization, opening, repetition and section.

The dome studies continue the same behaviour:

solid / void  
striated  
centralized  
stepped

So the dome is not just an object added on top of the building. It is one of the places where the project tests what happens when a volume is made to change.

There is also a clear difference between the centre and the outside.

The composition has a dominant centre and a differentiated field around it. The historical references helped this direction, but the sheet itself already shows centrality, multiplicity and hierarchy as architectural operations.

The building also has a strong threshold.

The entrance is treated as depth, not simply as a hole in a wall. The Iranian / Ilkhanid reference enters here through spatial depth, layered threshold and geometric articulation rather than through a general "Iranian style". The existing project record makes the same distinction explicitly: Iranian knowledge should not become a decorative layer pasted onto another building.

The other historical references are also already separated by what they contribute.

Saint Peter is associated with axial and cruciform organization, hierarchy, monumental scale and the central dome.

Saint Basil is associated with multiplicity, differentiation, rhythm, geometric articulation and chromatic complexity.

Achaemenid architecture enters through column, proportion, ceremonial order and structural presence.

Ilkhanid architecture enters through threshold, vault, geometry, depth, light, shadow and surface articulation.

But none of these references, by themselves, explains the building.

That is the important part.

The actual object seems to sit in the space between them.

### What is certain

There is a transformed massing process.

There is a central architectural hierarchy.

There is multiplicity around a dominant centre.

There is a strong relationship between solid and void.

There is vertical differentiation.

There is radial and stepped geometry.

There is a deep threshold.

There is an attempt to make historical knowledge produce something new rather than reproduce a historical building.

### What is still open

Why exactly was the original mass split?

Why a cross?

Why does the centre need to dominate?

Why does multiplicity need to surround it?

Why is the dome stepped rather than simply curved?

Why is the building exposed through section?

What does the void actually do to the architecture?

Which of these decisions came from necessity, and which came from intuition?

Those questions are not weaknesses in the project.

They are where the real design thinking starts.
