# CITATION ARCHITECTURE

Three different citation directions exist here, and they don't share one format.

**MERIDIAN citing history.** Every historical Source (Saint Peter's, Saint Basil's, Ilkhanid architecture, Achaemenid Persepolis) gets cited the way its source class in RESEARCH_PROTOCOL demands — an F1 fact about a building cites differently than an I1 reading of what that building means.

**Someone citing MERIDIAN.** Use CITATION.cff at the repo root. Full stop. Not the README, not a screenshot of the board, not a paraphrase of the paper's abstract — the .cff file is the one canonical citation target, specifically so credit routes back to a single verifiable point no matter which Dossier someone actually read.

**A fork citing MERIDIAN.** A fork cites the method, and separately cites which Canon items it's building on top of, by number (Canon #1, #2, and so on) — not "inspired by MERIDIAN" as a vague gesture. Vague gestures are exactly how REJ-004's whole worry — someone lightly altering the work and resubmitting it without credit — actually happens.
